## Query

> The mediator sits between treatment and outcome but also receives a second incoming arrow from the top node; how does that arrangement follow from the theta-weighted counterfactual mediation model?

## Answer

The arrangement follows because the SCM writes the mediator as a function of both treatment and confounder, and writes the outcome as a function of treatment plus mediator contributions with separate theta coefficients. Because theta parameters attach to each parent input, the model can represent a direct treatment effect and a mediated effect while also adjusting for C entering M and Y. The extra incoming arrow to the mediator is therefore not decorative; it is required under the counterfactual setup to keep the M-to-Y effect from mixing mediation with confounding.

## Reasoning Chain

The graph shows the mediator receiving arrows from both treatment and the top confounder, and then sending an arrow to the outcome alongside a direct treatment-to-outcome edge. The SCM states that parent effects enter M and Y through additive theta-weighted terms, enabling counterfactual decomposition of direct versus mediated influence. This structural split is exactly what the math needs to separate the treatment’s path-specific effect from confounding through C.
